[HEADING_2] H2: What Are the Three Categories of Water Damage?
[NORMAL_TEXT] The IICRC S500 standard, the main industry reference for water damage work, sorts water by how contaminated it is. The category decides how the job is handled.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 1, clean water: broken supply lines, burst pipes, overflowing sinks, rainwater. Lowest risk if handled quickly.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 2, gray water: washing machine and dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow without waste, sump pump failures. Contains contaminants that can make people sick.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 3, black water: sewage, creek and flood water from outside, and any water left standing long enough to grow bacteria. Porous materials it touches usually have to be removed.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Clean water turns into gray or black water when it sits. A pipe leak left for several days can become a Category 3 job, which is one more reason to start extraction early.
