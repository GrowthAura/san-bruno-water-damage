San Bruno Neighborhoods We Serve

San Bruno Park and Downtown

The city's oldest area, around San Mateo Avenue and the downtown blocks, has homes from the 1920s through the 1950s. Common calls here are burst galvanized pipes, cast iron drain leaks and sewer lateral backups.

Belle Air and Lomita Park

These low-lying neighborhoods near SFO and San Bruno Creek see storm water, street flooding and backups when heavy rain lines up with high tides. Garages and ground floors take the most damage.

Crestmoor and Shelter Creek

Mid-century ranches on hillside lots and the Shelter Creek condominiums. Crawl space water, downhill runoff and unit-to-unit leaks are the main problems here.

Rollingwood

Ranch homes from the 1950s and 1960s on hilly streets near I-280. Crawl spaces, roof leaks from wind-driven rain and older plumbing are common.

Portola Highlands

Larger 1960s homes in the western hills backing onto open space. Runoff, crawl spaces and fog-related moisture show up often.

Mills Park and Huntington Park

Some of the city's first suburban neighborhoods, close to El Camino Real. Older pipes, hardwood floors and slab or hidden leaks are typical calls.

Pacific Heights

Hillside homes on the western edge of town where storm runoff and foggy conditions combine.

Tanforan and Bayhill

Shopping, offices and apartment buildings near the San Bruno BART station, where commercial and multi-unit water damage is the main concern.
